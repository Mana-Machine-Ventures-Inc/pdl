# Programmatic Design Language: Manifesto

We are learning better ways to build **coherent product UI** and to help others do the same. Through that work we are coming to value:

---

**Source of truth in the open**  
over truth locked in proprietary canvases or one engineer’s head.

*A design system should be inspectable, diffable, and shareable — something a team can reason about together, not a black box.*

---

**Systems that compose**  
over one-off screens that happen to look similar.

*Reuse is a property of structure and rules, not of copy-paste. Small, combinable pieces beat heroic bespoke layouts.*

---

**Explicit contracts**  
over tribal knowledge and “you’ll know it when you see it.”

*Names, parameters, tokens, rules, and boundaries should be stated clearly enough that humans **and** tools can rely on them.*

---

**Working artifacts**  
over perfect documents nobody ships.

*Sketches, previews, generated output, and incremental truth beat a frozen spec that never meets reality.*

---

**Ownership of the work**  
over a lifetime tenancy inside one vendor’s canvas.

*People should keep **their** files: sources they can version, diff, fork, move and extend. Commercial tools are powerful for creation and collaboration, but are poor long-term stewards for a design repository.*

---

**Kindness to the next reader**  
over cleverness for its own sake.

*The next person might be you, six months later — or someone who never sat in the room. Clarity is a form of respect.*

---

*This manifesto is intent and philosophy — deliberately short. It does not prescribe a language, a file format, or a toolchain; those are choices that should serve these principles, not the other way around.*
